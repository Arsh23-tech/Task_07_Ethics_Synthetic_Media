## Important Source-Use Note

The Phase A ethical analysis is primarily grounded in the author's own Task 6 experiment and constructed thought experiments, as required by the assignment. The external sources above are used specifically for the **Mitigation Landscape** and governance portions of the analysis.

The Task 6 experiment did **not** experimentally test C2PA credential survival through re-encoding, screen recording, or social-platform uploads. The Phase A discussion of provenance therefore relies on the external C2PA and regulatory sources rather than presenting those tests as completed experiments.

Similarly, the Task 6 video was not submitted to an automated detector because the HeyGen free tier did not permit MP4 download. The **98% AI-generated result applies to the ElevenLabs audio only**.
